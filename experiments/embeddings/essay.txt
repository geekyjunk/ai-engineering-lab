Technology has changed the way people communicate, work, and learn.
The internet allows people to access information almost instantly.
However, constant access to information can also create problems.
People may become distracted by notifications and social media.

Artificial intelligence is another major technological development.
AI systems can process large amounts of information and assist people
with tasks such as writing, programming, research, and analysis.

Technology can improve productivity, but productivity does not always
mean that people are happier. Finding a balance between technology and
human activities is therefore important.
